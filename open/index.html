Conversion settings

Use the exact model ID your endpoint expects (on OpenRouter, with the provider prefix). Models the endpoint lists appear as suggestions.

The menu lists the newest open-weight DeepSeek, GPT OSS, Gemma and Qwen VL models your endpoint offers. If it has none of them, it lists all of its models. Your provider bills you at its price.

More options

For OCR, choose a page range separately before extraction.

Books: Open Library, Crossref and Google Books. Articles, chapters, papers, reports and theses: Crossref. Films, TV and radio, podcasts, recordings, software, datasets, maps and websites or digital projects: Wikidata. A DOI is checked in Crossref. Only the title, first creator, year, identifiers, series or site name and web address are sent, never the citation text. Suggestions only fill missing fields, after you review them.

From bibliography to Zotero

Add text, a TXT/DOCX/PDF document or a PNG/JPG image. Check where each citation begins, then convert with the AI service you choose under Model endpoint: OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral, Together AI, a model on your own computer, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Your provider bills conversion and Vision OCR. How long it keeps what you send depends on its policies. Check the original citation against each result before importing into Zotero.

Files and results stay in this tab. Text sent for conversion and page images sent for OCR go from your browser straight to your chosen endpoint. Nothing passes through this website. Optional catalog lookups send the title, first creator, year, identifiers and web address of each converted entry to Open Library, Crossref, Google Books or Wikidata. Some sources are not covered by any catalog; the result says so. Download your results before closing or reloading the tab.
